Manifesto

Every menstruating body is different. Generic period apps show you a textbook average — a fictional 28-day cycle that doesn't belong to anyone.

CycleSync starts from the opposite end. We treat your data as the only source of truth. The engine watches your symptoms, your biometrics, your sleep, your mood — and draws a map that's only true for you.

That map stays on your device. Forever. Because cycle data is among the most sensitive there is — and the simplest defense is to never have it leave your phone in the first place.

What CycleSync does
01

Your personal cycle map

Symptoms, mood, energy and sleep overlaid onto your phases. After three cycles, the patterns sharpen. After six, it's a tool no generic app can match — because no generic app sees your body.

02

A statistical engine, not a vibe

Pearson correlations, chi-squared independence tests, cyclicity detection. Built with Apple's Accelerate framework, gated at p < 0.05. If a pattern isn't significant, the engine stays quiet.

03

Apple Watch in the loop

HRV, sleep, temperature, resting heart rate — read from HealthKit, correlated with your phase. A native watchOS app and complications for quick logging without reaching for your phone.

Built for

Anyone who menstruates and wants their cycle to make sense.

We built CycleSync starting from three communities where the cycle effect is impossible to ignore — but the engine works for everyone.

Menstrual migraine
62% of migraineurs report cycle-linked attacks. Map your trigger window — and the biometrics that drift before it.
PMDD
3–8% of women, often dismissed for years. The Cycle Map distinguishes hormonal symptoms from random ones with statistical evidence.
Perimenopause
Cycles get irregular. Symptoms compound. CycleSync tracks the change as it happens, in your own data, with your own baseline.
Anyone curious
Energy, sleep, performance, cognition, skin, digestion — all phase-modulated. If you menstruate and want the map, the map is here.
Privacy by architecture

Cycle data stays on your phone.

Period and reproductive data has been subpoenaed, sold, and weaponised in recent years. We took the opposite stance from line one of code: there is no backend. There can't be a leak from a server that doesn't exist.

  • Account required No
  • Cloud sync of cycle data No
  • Analytics SDK No
  • Advertising trackers No
  • Data sold or shared No
  • HealthKit access Read-only
  • Where AI runs On-device
