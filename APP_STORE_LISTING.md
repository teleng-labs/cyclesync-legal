### Description (max 4000 chars)

CycleSync is a private cycle wellness app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

Track your menstrual cycle, symptoms, energy, mood, and daily context without creating an account and without sending your personal cycle data to a backend. CycleSync is designed for people who want useful cycle awareness without cloud sync, ads, analytics, or tracking.

Use CycleSync to:

- Log period days, symptoms, energy, mood, and reflections.
- See cycle forecasts based on your local history.
- Follow Cycle Battery signals for energy-aware planning.
- Build gentle daily protocols around movement, recovery, focus, and routine.
- Review biometric context from Apple Health with read-only permission.
- Use Apple Watch for quick cycle and wellness context.
- Explore optional on-device AI reflections.

Privacy is the core feature.

CycleSync runs on device. There is no CycleSync account, no CycleSync backend, no analytics SDK, no advertising tracker, and no data broker sharing. HealthKit access is read-only and permission-based.

Optional local AI:

CycleSync can use Apple on-device Foundation Models where available. You may also choose to download a local Gemma 3 4B model, approximately 4.1 GB, from Hugging Face for deeper on-device reflections. Your cycle logs, Health data, prompts, and AI history are not sent to CycleSync servers.

Important wellness notice:

CycleSync is for general wellness, self-reflection, and routine planning. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, prevention, monitoring, or cure. Do not use CycleSync for medical, emergency, fertility-critical, contraceptive, pregnancy-risk, clinical, insurance, employment, or safety-critical decisions. For health concerns, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
